clip-factory

A personal video-publishing tool.

clip-factory is a small private tool used by the operator of the YouTube channel Speed Unhinged to edit short highlight clips and upload them to their own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

It is not a public service: it does not offer accounts, does not sign in other users, and only accesses the channel owned by its operator.
